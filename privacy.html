Compressly Privacy Policy

Last Updated & Effective: January 1, 2026 | App: Compressly (Android)

1. Executive Summary & Privacy Philosophy

Compressly is built upon strict Privacy-by-Design principles. The application operates as a standalone, on-device utility designed to compress and optimize photos and images. Compressly does not upload, transmit, store, or sell any of your media files or personal information to third parties, remote servers, or cloud infrastructure.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

We believe the best way to secure user data is to never collect it. As such:

  • No Photo Uploads: Photos and graphics never leave your physical smartphone.
  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • No Location Data: Compressly never accesses your device's GPS or network location.
  • No Advertising Trackers: There are no third-party ad networks, fingerprinting SDKs, or behavioral trackers embedded in the app.

3. Android Device Permissions

To perform its core functions, Compressly requests minimal, scoped Android operating system permissions:

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Needed strictly so you can browse and select the photos you want to optimize.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / Scoped Storage: Needed to save the newly compressed photos to your phone's gallery or specified export folder.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE: Used during long-running batch photo compressions to prevent the Android operating system from interrupting processing when the screen is locked.

4. EXIF & Geolocation Metadata Sanitation

Photos captured by modern smartphones often contain sensitive EXIF metadata, including exact GPS coordinates and device hardware numbers. Compressly provides built-in metadata sanitization toggles, enabling users to strip all sensitive geographical tags before sharing compressed photos online.
