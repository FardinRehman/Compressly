Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Compressly, compatibility, photo modes, and privacy.

Is Compressly completely offline and safe to use?
Yes, 100%! Compressly requires no internet access or external server connections. Every compression algorithm runs locally on your smartphone's CPU/GPU, ensuring that your photos and personal images never leak to any cloud servers.
How does Compressly achieve 80-90% file size reduction without visible quality loss?
Compressly utilizes advanced perceptual psychovisual optimization (SSIM tuning) and modern quantization heuristics. It removes microscopic high-frequency image noise that human eyes cannot perceive while preserving sharp edges, colors, and textures.
Can I specify an exact file size, such as compressing under 2 MB for email or portals?
Yes! The Target Size mode allows you to type your desired maximum size (in KB or MB). Compressly automatically calculates the optimal resolution and bitrate to hit that target without exceeding it.
How do I install the direct APK on my Android phone?
Click the "Download Direct APK" button to download `base.apk`. Once downloaded, tap the notification or open your Downloads folder and tap the APK file. If prompted, enable "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager. The app is 100% digitally signed and safe.
What Android versions are supported?
Compressly supports Android 7.0 (Nougat, API Level 24) and all modern versions up to Android 14 and Android 15. It is compiled with 64-bit architecture support for ultra-fast processing on modern chipsets.
Will compression overwrite my original photos?
By default, Compressly creates a dedicated "Compressly" album in your gallery and saves the optimized versions there, leaving your originals completely untouched. You can also choose to replace the original if you want to immediately free up space.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

At Compressly, we prioritize user privacy above everything else. This Privacy Policy details how Compressly operates on Android devices and why your personal photos remain 100% safe.

1. 100% On-Device Processing

Compressly does not transmit your photos or images to any external servers. All compression, resizing, decoding, and encoding operations happen strictly in local device memory.

2. No Account & Zero Personal Data Collection

Compressly does not require account creation, logins, emails, phone numbers, or passwords. We do not collect names, contact lists, GPS locations, or browsing activity.

3. Device Permissions Used

  • Storage / Media Access: Required solely to read the photos you choose to compress and to save the resulting optimized photos back to your gallery.

4. Metadata & EXIF Handling

When compressing photos, you have the option to strip EXIF metadata (camera model, GPS coordinates, timestamps) to protect your location privacy when sharing files on social media.
